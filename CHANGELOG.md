# Changelog

## 0.3.2

- Show Stockfish's suggestions as arrows on the review board.
- Keep move suggestions compact and open each continuation on demand.

## 0.3.1

- Identify the best line Stockfish found and the move played in the game.
- Compare alternatives from the position before a move and preview its actual continuation.

## 0.3.0

- Review a finished game on the same board. Jump to any position, see Stockfish's evaluation, and play through up to three suggested lines.
- Review runs offline and keeps the original game intact.

## 0.2.2

- Keep toolbar labels readable in narrow sidebars, including longer translations.

## 0.2.1

- Choose the interface language in Settings. English, Russian, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, and Simplified Chinese are available.
- Puzzle hints highlight the square more clearly and name it below the board.

## 0.2.0

- Added 9,781 offline Lichess puzzles, with theme and rating filters.
- Show hints, play through the solution, or retry a puzzle from the starting position.
- Track solved puzzles locally. Switching to puzzles pauses the current game so it can be resumed.

## 0.1.1

- Drag pieces independently of their squares and board markings.
- Added random color selection and a clearer checkmate result.
- Show draw claims when available and highlight eligible moves for intended claims.
- Expanded Stockfish to 16 difficulty presets while preserving existing selections.

## 0.1.0

- Added a movable chess view for desktop VS Code, with click, drag-and-drop, and keyboard play.
- Added offline Stockfish 19 Lite with six difficulty levels, color selection, and adjustable thinking time.
- Added local two-player games, optional Fischer clocks, and automatic board rotation.
- Added promotion, draw claims, automatic draw rules, takebacks, move history, and game restoration.
- Added three board themes and two piece styles.
